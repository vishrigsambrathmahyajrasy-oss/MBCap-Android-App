Auto bots (up to 10 at once) Simulated wallet

To add a bot, open any coin (Markets, search or New Launches) and use its Auto tab. Each coin can have one bot.

The bot buys, sells at your take-profit, then buys again when the price dips by the re-entry % and repeats. A stop-loss ends the bot for that coin. Every buy and every sell pays a 0.06% fee. It runs in this page with a simulated $1,000 wallet and sample prices that move every 2 seconds, and stops when you close the page.

    Practice mode: trades are simulated and no money moves. Prices come from live DEX data when it has loaded.

    Autopilot Practice mode

    Give one approval, and Autopilot picks coins by itself from the top 50 and from new tokens with strong buyers, buys, sells at your take-profit, re-enters on dips, and repeats within the limits below, without asking each time. It runs only while this page is open and uses the practice wallet. Trading 24 hours with the app closed needs a server agent that is not built yet. Rules are simple filters, not predictions, and can lose money.
